## What does ChatGPT Plus cost directly from OpenAI?

The current official benchmark is:

> **ChatGPT Plus: $20 per month, billed monthly.**

The Plus subscription provides broader access than the free plan, including higher model limits, faster responses, priority access during busy periods, voice conversations, image generation, file uploads, file analysis, and Deep Research tools where available. The exact models and limits can change as OpenAI updates ChatGPT.

There are two details that often get missed.

First, **Plus is not an annual subscription**. OpenAI’s help documentation says there is currently no annual billing option and no official way to prepay multiple months in advance for ChatGPT Plus.

Second, **ChatGPT Plus does not include API credits**. If you use the OpenAI API for a website, software project, automation workflow, or developer tool, API usage is billed separately from the ChatGPT subscription.

That means the $20 price is for the ChatGPT application itself. It is not a bundle that includes unlimited API calls.

## Free vs Go vs Plus vs Pro

The cheapest plan is not automatically the right one. The difference is mainly about how often you use ChatGPT and how demanding your tasks are.

### Free

The Free plan is suitable for basic questions, short writing tasks, occasional image generation, and light experimentation. It avoids a monthly charge, but limits are lower and access to advanced features may be more restricted.

Choose Free if you use ChatGPT occasionally and do not mind waiting or switching tools when you reach a limit.

### Go

OpenAI introduced Go as a lower-cost paid option. In the United States, the published price is $8 per month. It is positioned between Free and Plus, offering more messages, uploads, and image creation than the free tier at a lower price than Plus.

Go may make sense if your main problem is hitting the free usage limit, but you do not need the broader reasoning, research, coding, and long-form workflow capabilities associated with Plus.

### Plus

Plus is the middle option for regular users. It is the plan most people mean when they search for ChatGPT Plus pricing.

It makes the most sense for:

- Writers working with documents or research notes.
- Students who need explanations, study support, or file analysis.
- Developers using ChatGPT for debugging and planning.
- Freelancers who use image generation, voice, or research tools.
- Professionals who open ChatGPT every day.
- Users who want a more consistent experience during peak traffic.

At $20 per month, Plus is twice the published U.S. price of Go but far below Pro. Whether the difference is worthwhile depends on whether you will use the additional tools and higher limits regularly.

### Pro

OpenAI’s current published consumer information lists Pro at $200 per month. It is intended for intensive users who need higher limits and access to the most advanced capabilities.

For most casual users, Pro is difficult to justify. If your work depends on running many complex tasks, using advanced reasoning frequently, or working with large volumes of content, the higher limits may matter. For ordinary writing, questions, brainstorming, and occasional research, Plus is usually the more proportionate choice.

## How WildAI’s ChatGPT subscription process works

WildAI’s help center describes a four-step process:

1. Select ChatGPT Plus or another available plan.
2. Log in to the ChatGPT account you want to use.
3. Submit the required account information.
4. Confirm the email shown on the order and complete payment.

The help documentation says activation usually takes around 5 to 15 minutes after payment, although actual processing can vary. It also warns users not to pay again immediately if the order is still processing.

The process is important because WildAI is not simply providing a shared ChatGPT account. The stated flow is intended to activate the subscription on the customer’s own account.

However, users should read the instructions carefully before submitting any authentication information. WildAI’s documentation describes copying a JSON session value from a browser session in some subscription flows. That type of information can represent an active login session and should be treated as sensitive.

Do not submit:

- Your ChatGPT password.
- One-time verification codes.
- Recovery codes.
- Backup authentication codes.
- Credentials from a work or company account unless your organization explicitly allows it.

If a third-party flow requests a session token or cookie, make sure you understand what it is used for, how long it is retained, and how the session can be revoked afterward. A payment service is not automatically a security service.

## Single-month or multi-month subscription?

WildAI’s help content distinguishes between single-month and long-term subscription options.

A single-month option is intended for one month of service. A long-term option covers multiple months and may offer a lower average monthly price. The same help page warns that some multi-month plans may involve continued monthly processing and that canceling the subscription from the official ChatGPT page can affect the remaining prepaid period.

Before selecting a multi-month option, confirm:

- Whether the displayed price is the total amount or the monthly amount.
- Whether the subscription renews automatically.
- Whether later months are activated in advance or processed month by month.
- Whether a second authentication step is needed later.
- What happens if the upstream account is restricted.
- How unused months are handled after cancellation.
- Whether the final checkout amount includes currency conversion or fees.

A lower monthly average is only useful if you are confident that you will use the service for the entire period. Paying for three months to save a few dollars per month is not a bargain if you only need Plus for a short project.
